Reading the brief

Evidence before interpretation

Each public observation is dated and linked where possible. Secondary market snapshots are labelled separately from issuer and regulatory records.

1 · What happened

The dated event or company fact, its source, and what is confirmed versus alleged or estimated.

2 · Cause and transmission

Why the event occurred where evidence supports it, and the path through rates, costs, demand, trade, regulation or risk.

3 · Likely and conditional effects

Direct effects are separated from second-order possibilities. Uncertain causal links are labelled rather than stated as fact.

4 · What changes the next decision

Each issue states the confirming signals, invalidating evidence and dated condition DEDL is waiting for before it changes a classification.
