Cybersecurity · global industry exposure

SafePay ransomware and active edge-device risks

On 20 October, CISA, FBI, U.S. Secret Service and international partners issued a SafePay ransomware advisory. The agencies say SafePay has affected at least 600 organizations across multiple critical-infrastructure sectors since August 2024. They describe initial access involving credential exposure, brute-force attacks and exploited internet-facing VPN/edge devices, and recommend prompt patching, MFA and restricting administrative privilege. This is a cross-industry operational and recovery risk; no attribution beyond the advisory is asserted. CISA advisory · 20 Oct

Singapore's CSA separately listed active exploitation of an Ivanti Connect Secure vulnerability and advisories for vulnerabilities in Advantech ADAM-3600 EdgeLink and Next.js. Operators using these products should follow the respective vendor and CSA remediation guidance; the public brief omits exploit details. Singapore CSA alerts · updated 23 Oct
