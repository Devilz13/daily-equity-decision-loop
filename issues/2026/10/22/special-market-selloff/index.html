Selloff, not a verified broad-market crash

At the US opening, Reuters reported the Dow down 0.13%, S&P 500 down 0.81%, and Nasdaq Composite down 1.48%. These reflect meaningful selling, particularly in technology, but these opening changes alone do not establish a broad-market crash. They are time-specific figures, not a live or closing quote.
