Issue 05913 November 2026

Microsoft 365: some regions are coming back online; outage continues

Evidence reviewed at 10:58 SGT on 13 November 2026. Partial recovery; no all-clear.

What changed

Microsoft’s 09:48 SGT (01:48 UTC) update reported encouraging restart results and ongoing stability checks. At 10:10 SGT (02:10 UTC), it said it was adding infrastructure capacity where possible as regions came back online. The public mirror still listed MO1550821 as degraded. Microsoft has not confirmed full restoration or a reliable restoration time in the evidence reviewed. Public mirror of the Microsoft 365 Service Health incident.

What can still fail

Exchange Online is also tracked under EX1550543. Outlook mailbox access and email delivery can still be affected. Microsoft’s scope note says Exchange-dependent Teams functions such as calendars and scheduling may fail while core Teams chat and meetings remain largely functional. Some Microsoft 365 Copilot and SharePoint features can also be affected. Cisco ThousandEyes observed global connectivity errors; CNA cited 250 Singapore Outlook reports at 07:05 SGT. This is a local self-report count, not a count of confirmed impacted users or a verdict on all Azure services. University of Pennsylvania incident mirror · Independent network monitoring · CNA reporting.

Why it happened: what is known

Microsoft traced servers stopping work to overload in the process that sets up secure connections. It is testing a targeted change and reverting a recent network change that coincided with the start of impact. That timing does not confirm the underlying trigger, and no cyberattack is established by the evidence. Incident updates · Contemporaneous reporting.

What to do now

If email or Teams scheduling fails and the matter is urgent, use an alternative your organisation approves and confirm receipt. Check Microsoft’s public service health page or your IT team for current status. Read the initial bulletin for the incident timeline.
