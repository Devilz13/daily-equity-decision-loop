Issue 06113 November 2026

Microsoft 365 outage disrupts some Singapore government email; Singpass operational

Evidence checked at 13:05 SGT on 13 November 2026.

Singapore: what changed

GovTech told The Straits Times that some government systems, including email, were affected and that it was working with Microsoft. Its statement said Singpass was unaffected, emergency services were operating normally, and public officers could still use Teams for internal communications. Singpass’s own status page listed its login service as operational at 12:52 SGT (04:52 UTC). GovTech statement as reported by The Straits Times · Official Singpass status.

If you need to contact an agency

Email responses may be delayed. For urgent enquiries, use the call centre or another channel listed on the relevant agency’s official website. Singapore Courts said online filing and notifications generated by its filing systems were still working, although messages sent to the court could be affected. IRAS pointed taxpayers to Live Chat or myTax Mail in myTax Portal. These are specific agency notices; they do not establish a government-wide loss of service. Agency notices reported on 13 November.

Global recovery is still unconfirmed

Microsoft reported that traffic adjustments helped some regions temporarily but did not produce sustained recovery. In the MO1550821 update timestamped 12:56 SGT (04:56 UTC) by a public service-health mirror, Microsoft reported an increase in internal requests to back-end servers during its mitigation work and said it was still investigating why. The public Exchange Online mirror still listed related incident EX1550543 as degraded. Earlier independent network monitoring saw widespread Outlook timeouts at 11:00 SGT. No confirmed restoration time or definitive cause was available at this check.
