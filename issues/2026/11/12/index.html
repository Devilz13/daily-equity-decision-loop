NVIDIA · supplier bottlenecks, governance and valuation

NVIDIA’s board announced an independent investigation on 8 November, saying it had no reason at that point to believe national security or company secrets were compromised. Huang acknowledged personal misconduct that day. Claims about foreign intelligence, shipment facilitation and a separate internal relationship remain allegations. Reuters reported a late 11 November US board meeting and a request from two US senators to preserve export records. Neither a board decision nor an export violation was independently confirmed at this review.

NVIDIA reported Q2 FY27 revenue of US$96.2bn (+106% year on year), including US$89.0bn in Data Center (+117%, about 92.5% of total), a 75.0% GAAP gross margin and US$2.46 diluted GAAP EPS. Its August guidance of US$108bn ±2% for Q3 assumed no China Data Center compute revenue. That makes a direct hit to the old guidance less automatic; broader controls, customer delays and key-person risk still matter.

The US cash close was US$173.73 on 11 November (+2.87% from US$168.89), after two down sessions from US$186.64 on 6 November. Stock Analysis recorded US$158.76 at 19:59 EST on 11 November (08:59 SGT on 12 November), down 8.62% from the cash close. This was extended-hours trading, which can overstate the move. Reported cash volume on 10 November was about 429 million shares, 2.4 times the 6 November volume of 180 million in the same series. The cash close remained about 20% below the 50-session SMA (US$217.12) and 15% below the 200-session SMA (US$204.47). The 10 November low of US$165.89 is a test, not a floor; a regular-session recovery above the 11 November high near US$177.54 would improve the near-term picture. A cash close below US$165.89 with unusually heavy volume would strengthen the breakdown case.

Four published quarterly GAAP diluted EPS figures (US$1.30, US$1.76, US$2.39, US$2.46) sum to US$7.91. That implies 22.0× trailing GAAP P/E at US$173.73 and 20.1× at US$158.76. The vendor’s 14.35× forward P/E at the cash close implies roughly US$12.11 in estimated EPS, 53% above trailing EPS; its exact period and adjustment basis were not independently verified. Historical GAAP EPS can include investment gains and other non-operating items. A falling price reduces the quoted multiple only if the EPS denominator holds.

Research stance: WATCH / wait for confirmation. The multiple is more attractive if earnings rise as expected, but it does not establish a margin of safety if earnings estimates fall or investors assign a lower multiple. A credible board disclosure and resilient guidance, cash conversion and shipments would strengthen the case. Illustrative EPS and multiple pairs give US$120 (US$8 × 15), US$180 (US$10 × 18), and US$264 (US$12 × 22); these are sensitivities, not targets. The next scheduled earnings release is about 05:20 SGT on 18 November (17 November US time), with the call at 06:00 SGT.
