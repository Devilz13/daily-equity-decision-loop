NVIDIA stock watch: what the dip does and does not tell us

The lower price is real. A bargain is unproven. Facts, assumptions and what to check next.

Confirmed and reportedPrice reactionEarnings and valuationWhat would change the view

What the sources establish

NVIDIA’s board announced an independent investigation on 8 November after reports of a possible foreign intelligence effort targeting Huang. The board said at that point it had no reason to believe US national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised. Huang acknowledged personal misconduct in a separate message. Neither statement confirms espionage or an export violation. NVIDIA board statement, 8 November · NVIDIA: Huang’s 8 November message to employees.

On 11 November US time, Reuters reported, citing a person familiar with the matter, that the board was meeting to consider whether to remove Huang. Reuters also relayed a separate New York Times allegation of a relationship with Kimberly Powell, NVIDIA’s healthcare vice president and a direct report. That allegation has not been independently verified here. The public sources reviewed do not establish that it triggered the meeting, that the board voted to remove Huang, or that an export violation occurred. A reported request by US senators to preserve export records is not a finding. Reuters, 11 November US: board meeting and New York Times reporting · Reuters: reported request to preserve export records.

Business results and valuation

NVIDIA’s most recent reported quarter, ended 26 July, had revenue of US$96.2bn (+106% year over year), Data Center revenue of US$89.0bn (+117%), and a 75.0% GAAP gross margin. Data Center was about 92.5% of sales, so this growth also shows concentration. GAAP diluted EPS was US$2.46; the company’s adjusted figure was US$2.22. Quarterly operating cash flow was US$24.08bn versus US$50.34bn in Q1; issuer-defined free cash flow fell to US$21.34bn from US$48.55bn. That is a sequential cash conversion warning to recheck, not proof of a permanent decline. Its August Q3 revenue outlook of US$108bn ±2% assumed no China data-center compute revenue. Wider restrictions, delays or weaker customer spending could still affect later earnings. NVIDIA: Q2 FY27 results and guidance · SEC EDGAR: NVIDIA Form 10-Q for period ended 26 July.

The four reported GAAP diluted EPS figures of US$1.30, US$1.76, US$2.39 and US$2.46 sum to US$7.91. At the prices above, that is 22.0× trailing GAAP P/E at the cash close and 20.1× at the after-hours snapshot. This compares prices against past earnings, which can include non-operating gains. It does not establish next-year profit. NVIDIA: Q3 FY26 results · NVIDIA: Q4 FY26 results · NVIDIA: Q1 FY27 results · NVIDIA: Q2 FY27 results and guidance.

What would change the assessment?

Opportunity case: credible board and succession disclosures, maintained guidance, sustained margins and evidence that customer orders translate into shipments and cash. The prior quarter’s growth makes that case plausible; it does not prove it.

Risk case: a wider export restriction, customer spending delays, weaker cash conversion, or a sustained technical breakdown. A leadership decision could also affect regulatory and customer relationships. These are possible outcomes, not confirmed effects.
