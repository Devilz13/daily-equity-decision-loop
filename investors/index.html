What we can and cannot infer

What a filing proves

Specific reportable securities and share counts as of an explicit quarter-end. A change is observable only by comparing successive verified filings, adjusted for splits, share classes and amendments.

What a filing does not prove

The manager's current position, conviction, cost basis, daily trading, performance, private holdings, leverage, derivative hedges or reason for owning an issuer. Public disclosures cannot reconstruct a complete strategy.

Investment philosophy versus interpretation

Documented public statements and firm descriptions are presented with sources. DEDL-derived research questions are explicitly labelled as interpretations, not quotations or proprietary manager rules.

How this relates to DEDL

Manager holdings provide public case studies and candidate context. The separate private DEDL methodology, validation, trade approvals, portfolio exposures and account data are not stored or published here.
